Four moves, no queue theatre.

Madis runs like a lab bench, not a counter. You do the making, the shop does the weighing.

1

Take a cup

Start at the top of the line and pick up a cup. Everything after this point is your design.

2

Fill it yourself

Pull the lever on the self serve machines and swirl in as much frozen yogurt as you want. One flavour, or a twist of two.

3

Hit the toppings bar

Fresh fruit, sauces, crunch and little extras. Spoon on what you like, in whatever order you like.

4

Weigh and pay

Take your cup to the counter. It goes on the scale, you pay for what you built, and it is yours.

The toppings bar

This is where a plain cup becomes your cup. Everything below was seen at the bar in the shop.

Photo is illustrative of a self serve toppings bar.

Fresh fruit

Strawberries, blueberries, blackberries and raspberries. The cold, bright layer that cuts through the swirl.

Sauces

Chocolate sauce, matcha sauce, Dubai sauce, Toblerone sauce and nocciola. Poured over the top, as much as the cup can hold.

Crunch and extras

Popping bobas, caramel beads, brownie pieces, granola, shredded coconut, freeze dried raspberries, crushed nuts and cereal pearls. Texture, snap and the last spoonful surprise.

Inside the lab

A small shop with a few things you will remember after the cup is gone.

The red photobooth

One wall in the shop is a red walled photobooth, made for the cup in your hand and the people you brought with you.

“Say I Love You”

Written on the mirror in the shop. Check your swirl, read the mirror, take the photo.

Dogs are welcome

Bring yours. Madis is a dog friendly stop on Calle de Alcala, so nobody waits outside.
